Device: Industrial Motor Controller (IMC-100)

Safety
- Disconnect power before opening enclosures.
- If temperature exceeds 80°C, stop the device and inspect ventilation.

Common failure modes and symptoms
1) Bearing wear / misalignment
   - Symptom: increasing vibration RMS, especially persistent over multiple hours.
   - Checks:
     - Inspect shaft alignment
     - Check bearing lubrication
     - Listen for abnormal noise under load
   - Recommended actions:
     - Re-lubricate bearings if dry
     - Replace bearings if vibration continues after lubrication

2) Overheating / cooling obstruction
   - Symptom: rising temperature trend, thermal spikes, or temperature persistently above 75°C.
   - Checks:
     - Inspect cooling fan operation
     - Clean vents and filters
     - Verify ambient temperature and airflow
   - Recommended actions:
     - Clear obstructions and replace clogged filters
     - Validate fan RPM and power supply

3) Electrical overcurrent
   - Symptom: current draw increases unexpectedly; may correlate with mechanical drag.
   - Checks:
     - Inspect wiring, terminals, and insulation
     - Verify load conditions
     - Check for jammed mechanical components
   - Recommended actions:
     - Reduce load and retest
     - Replace damaged wiring
